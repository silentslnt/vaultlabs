Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

VaultLabs ("we", "us", or "our") operates vaultlabs.dev. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or purchase our products.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when making a purchase:

  • Name and email address (collected by Whop, our payment processor)
  • Payment information (processed entirely by Whop — we never see your card details)
  • Communications you send us via email

We also collect limited data automatically via Cloudflare analytics:

  • Page views and general traffic data (anonymized)
  • Country-level location data
  • Browser and device type

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill your purchase and grant access to purchased content
  • To send you product updates and new content notifications (you may opt out at any time)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve our website and products

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Whop. We do not store or have access to your payment card information. Whop's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment data. You can review it at whop.com/privacy.

4. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for basic site functionality and Cloudflare's security and performance services. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to access our content.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with:

  • Whop — to process payments and manage product access
  • Cloudflare — to host and deliver our website

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@vaultlabs.dev.

8. Children's Privacy

Our products are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at hello@vaultlabs.dev.
